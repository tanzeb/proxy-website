How payment works

  • The price is ₹350 per month, or ₹3,600 per year (instead of ₹4,200 for twelve months), inclusive of applicable taxes, if any. All prices are in Indian Rupees.
  • Payment is in advance, monthly or yearly, by UPI AutoPay through Razorpay. You approve the mandate once in your UPI app; it is then debited each month, or each year on the yearly plan.
  • You subscribe from inside the app (the Order Summary & SPS button). If you subscribe during the free trial, your paid month starts when the trial ends, so no free days are lost.
  • You can cancel at any time, from the app or from your UPI app. Renewal stops, and Pro stays on until the end of the month, or year, you have paid for.
  • No refund is given for a period that has already started. See Refunds & cancellation.

Delivery

Proxy is software. Nothing is shipped. The app is downloaded from this website, and Proxy Pro is switched on in the app as soon as your trial starts or your payment is confirmed, normally within a few minutes.

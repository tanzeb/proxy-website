Refunds and cancellation

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Free trial

Proxy Pro starts with a 7-day free trial. No payment is taken to start it, so you can decide whether Pro is useful to you before paying anything.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel Proxy Pro at any time.
  • In the app: click Order Summary & SPS at the top, then Cancel subscription.
  • In your UPI app: open its AutoPay or Mandates section and cancel or pause the Proxy mandate.
  • Cancelling stops all future payments. Proxy Pro stays on until the end of the month, or year, you have already paid for, and then switches off. The free features of Proxy keep working, and your downloaded orders stay on your computer.

Refunds

  • No refund is given for a period that has already started. Proxy Pro is paid in advance, monthly or yearly; once a month (or, on the yearly plan, a year) has begun, its payment is not refunded, in full or in part, if you cancel during it.
  • If you were charged by mistake — charged twice for the same month, or charged after you had cancelled — write to us and we will refund the wrong charge in full.
  • Approved refunds are made to the account the payment came from, through Razorpay, normally within 5 to 7 working days.

How to ask

Write to saif_fundoo@yahoo.co.in with your registered mobile number and the date of the payment. We aim to reply within 2 working days.

Shipping

Proxy is software delivered over the internet. There is no physical delivery and so no shipping or return of goods.

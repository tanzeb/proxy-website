How it works

  1. Add a case. Case type, number and year, and the folder where its orders should go.
  2. Proxy checks the court's website. New orders are downloaded and filed for you.
  3. Open a case to see its orders, next dates, summaries and Suit Proceeding Sheet.

Summaries and the Suit Proceeding Sheet are prepared with AI from the text of the order. They are an aid to reading the order, not a substitute for it. Always check the order itself.

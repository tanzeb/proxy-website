Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information Proxy ("we", G6/15, Upper Ground Floor, Malviya Nagar, New Delhi 110017, India) handles when you use the Proxy app and Proxy Pro.

What stays on your computer

Your list of cases, the orders downloaded for them, your notes and edits in the Suit Proceeding Sheet, and your folders stay on your own computer. We do not receive your case list or your files. Without Proxy Pro, the app only talks to the Delhi High Court's website (to look up your cases, as any visitor would) and to GitHub (to check for app updates).

We do not collect your name, your email address, your contacts, your location, or your clients' details, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

How order text is handled

  • When an order is summarised, its text is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic, whose Claude AI model reads it and returns the summary. Anthropic processes the text on our behalf to provide that reading.
  • Orders of the Delhi High Court are public court records. To avoid reading the same order twice, we store the summary and reading of each order against a fingerprint of its text, and give that same result to any other Proxy Pro user who has the identical order. The stored reading is not linked to you or to your case list.
  • Only orders downloaded from the court's website for your tracked cases are sent. Your own documents are never sent.

How the cause list works

Our server reads the court's published cause lists once and keeps their text until the listed day has passed. Your app downloads that text and looks for your cases on your own computer. Your list of cases is never sent to us for this.

We never receive or store your UPI PIN, bank account or card details. We do not sell your information, and we share it only as set out above or where the law requires.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept while your account exists and for as long afterwards as we need for accounts, tax and legal records. Stored order readings are kept so that orders need not be read again.

Security

Connections between the app and our server are encrypted. Your sign-in is stored on your computer in encrypted form. Access to our database is restricted to our server. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices

  • You can use Proxy without Proxy Pro, in which case we collect nothing about you.
  • You can sign out of Proxy Pro in the app at any time.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your account information by writing to saif_fundoo@yahoo.co.in from, or quoting, your registered mobile number. We will delete it unless we must keep it by law.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.

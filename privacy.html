Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information Proxy ("we", G6/15, Upper Ground Floor, Malviya Nagar, New Delhi 110017, India) handles when you use the Proxy app and Proxy Pro.

What stays on your computer

The orders downloaded for your cases, your notes and edits in the Suit Proceeding Sheet, and your folders stay on your own computer. We do not receive your files. Without Proxy Pro, your list of cases stays on your computer too. Without Proxy Pro, the app only talks to the Delhi High Court's website (to look up your cases, as any visitor would), to GitHub (to check for app updates each time it starts) and to our server for one anonymous count, below.

Counting copies in use

Each time Proxy opens, it sends our server a random number made on your computer the first time the app ran, together with whether it is the Mac or Windows version and its version number. This tells us how many copies are in use and on which versions. It contains no name, mobile number, case, order or file, and it is not linked to your Proxy Pro account. Deleting the app's data on your computer makes a new random number.

How order text is handled

  • When an order is summarised, its text is sent to our server and from there to Anthropic, whose Claude AI model reads it and returns the summary. Anthropic processes the text on our behalf to provide that reading.
  • Orders of the Delhi High Court are public court records. To avoid reading the same order twice, we store the summary and reading of each order against a fingerprint of its text, and give that same result to any other Proxy Pro user who has the identical order. The stored reading is not linked to you or to your case list.
  • Only orders downloaded from the court's website for your tracked cases are sent. Your own documents are never sent.
  • To have summaries ready, we may download the orders of the cases on Proxy Pro users' lists from the court's website ourselves and have them read in advance in the same way. Those readings are stored like any other: against the order's fingerprint, not linked to you.

How the cause list works

Our server reads the court's published cause lists once and keeps their text until the listed day has passed. Your app downloads that text and looks for your cases on your own computer. This matching does not use the case numbers sent to us.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept while your account exists and for as long afterwards as we need for accounts, tax and legal records. Stored order readings are kept so that orders need not be read again. The anonymous copies-in-use count holds nothing about you and is kept while Proxy is offered.

Security

Connections between the app and our server are encrypted. Your sign-in is stored on your computer in encrypted form. Access to our database is restricted to our server. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices

  • You can use Proxy without Proxy Pro, in which case we collect nothing about you.
  • You can sign out of Proxy Pro in the app at any time.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your account information by writing to us by post (see Contact), quoting your registered mobile number. We will delete it unless we must keep it by law.
